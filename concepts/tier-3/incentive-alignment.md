# Incentive Alignment

> **The mechanisms ensuring that self-interested actions by Complementors naturally benefit the Orchestrator and the ecosystem.**

## Definition

**Short:** Revenue sharing, grants, recognition, data rights — the economic engine of governance that aligns self-interest with ecosystem interest.

## Constraints

- Misaligned incentives manifest slowly (quality decay, churn) and are expensive to reverse.
- Over-subsidisation creates dependency fragile in downturns.
