# Trust Mechanisms

> **Institutional or algorithmic systems that reduce transaction costs and perceived risk between actors who do not know each other.**

## Definition

**Short:** Reviews, escrow, certification, reputation scores: the institutional or algorithmic systems that enable low-friction value exchange among strangers.

## Constraints

- Algorithmic trust can be gamed (review fraud).
- Opaque trust algorithms erode the very trust they are meant to create.
